About Architronica

Straightforward. Quick.
Built properly.

Architronica is a design and build company based in North London. We design, plan and build extensions, full refurbishments, loft conversions and new homes for homeowners and small developers.

Our own team carries out the work, so one company is responsible for the whole project. We're straightforward with people, we get projects finished quickly, and we build to a high standard at a fair price.
