Questions

Before you get in touch

What happens after I fill in the form?

One of our team calls you, usually the same working day. It is a short call to understand your project. If it makes sense, we book a proper consultation.

We're still planning. Is it too early?

No. Early is the best time to talk. We can tell you what is possible, what needs planning permission and what it is likely to cost.

What if our budget doesn't match?

We will tell you straight. Often there is a way to phase the work or change the spec. If there isn't, you will hear that before you spend anything.

What areas do you cover?

London and Essex, including Hampstead, Kensington, Islington, Crouch End, Muswell Hill, Finchley, Barnet, Chigwell, Loughton, Ealing and Chiswick. Nearby? Ask us.

Do you do full design and build?

Yes. We take you from drawings and planning through to the finished build. If you already have plans, we can build from those too.
